      How roads work

      • Road paints one-way lanes in the direction you drag. With 2+ lanes, traffic keeps right: half the lanes go each way.
      • Where roads cross, or a road passes a station, cells become junctions (two-way). Robots only turn, change lanes or U-turn at junctions.
      • Use Junction to add turning points by hand, e.g. at the end of an aisle.
      • Stations and robots: next step. Existing ones are kept and shown.
